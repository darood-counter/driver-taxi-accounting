Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Driver Taxi Accounting respects your privacy. This policy explains what information may be collected and how it is used when you use our website and application.

Information we collect

How we use information

We use information to create and manage accounts, provide accounting features, maintain membership access, verify payments, provide support, prevent misuse and improve the service.

Data sharing

We may use service providers necessary to operate hosting, authentication, database, payment and support functions. We do not sell personal information.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect account and service data. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.
