What Driver's Accounting provides

Driver's Accounting is a cloud-based accounting application for taxi drivers. It helps users manage daily income and deductions, invoices, mileage, trips, bookings, targets, leaves, charge settings and accounting reports through an online dashboard.

Invoice ManagementCreate, update, review and print saved invoice records.
Accounting ReportsReview Daily, Monthly and Yearly accounting reports and totals.
Charge SetupMaintain date-based charge and rate settings used by accounting calculations.
Target SetupManage date-based targets and target slabs for driver accounting.
LeavesKeep track of driver leave records inside the accounting system.
MembershipMembership status, plan information and payment history are stored with the account.
Prices are shown in USD as configured for the application's membership plans. Paddle Checkout may display the applicable local currency, taxes or other checkout details based on the customer's location and the live Paddle catalog configuration.
