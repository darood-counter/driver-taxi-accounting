What Driver's Accounting provides

Driver's Accounting is a cloud-based accounting application for taxi drivers. The application brings invoice entry, invoice history, accounting reports, charge settings, targets and leave records together in one dashboard.

Invoice ManagementCreate, update, review and print saved invoice records. Invoice fields include income, deductions, Shift Trips, Bookings and Kilometers Hired.
Invoice HistoryFind saved invoices using the invoice history area, including date and invoice-number search/filter controls.
Daily / Monthly / Yearly ReportsReview accounting totals and invoice records by reporting period, including report details and invoice ledger information.
Charge SetupMaintain date-based charge settings used by invoice calculations, including RTA, Fuel and Booking rates.
Target SetupManage date-based target ranges and AED target amounts used when preparing invoices.
LeavesRecord driver leave selections and manage leave dates from the Leaves area.
MembershipView the current membership plan, status, start date, expiry and total paid information in the account.
Driver ProfileMaintain the driver's account/profile information and access the application's protected areas.
